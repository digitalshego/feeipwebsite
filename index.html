Our mission

Our roots run deep, but our love runs deeper.

F.E.E.I.P stands for Family, Education, Economic, Investment Plan — a commitment to keep the family close, lift up the next generation through education, and build shared economic strength that lasts.

For twenty-six gatherings the F.E.E.I.P family has come together to honor the people who came before us, celebrate the ones beside us, and make room for the ones still to come. This year we meet in Kissimee, FL for five days of shared meals, old stories, new faces, and the kind of laughter you only get around family.

How to book

  1. Call (866) 892-5890. Reservations for the block are by phone only, so have a card ready for the deposit.
  2. Give the group code FFR and reference RG-24939. Tell the agent you are with the F.E.E.I.P Family Reunion so you get the group rate.
  3. Book June 16–20, 2027. The block requires a minimum four-night stay; those exact dates cover it. Ask about earlier arrival or later departure if you want to extend.
  4. Note the $39 resort fee. It is charged by the resort on top of the room rate. Confirm with the agent whether it applies nightly or per stay.
  5. Save your confirmation number and let the reunion committee know you are coming through the contact page.
Rooms are released after May 7, 2027. After that date, the group code may no longer be honored and standard rates apply. Villas at Orange Lake have kitchens and multiple bedrooms, so consider sharing with cousins to split the cost.

Frequently asked questions

Good questions, quick answers

Don't see yours? Send it in from the contact page and we'll add it here.

When and where is the reunion?
June 16–20, 2027 at Holiday Inn Club Vacations at Orange Lake Resort in Kissimmee, Florida, about 20 minutes from Walt Disney World and 30–40 minutes from Orlando International Airport (MCO).
How do I book a room at the group rate?
Call (866) 892-5890, give group code FFR and reference RG-24939, and book June 16–20. The block requires a four-night minimum and closes on May 7, 2027. Full details are on the Hotel & Booking page.
Is there a resort fee?
Yes, the resort charges a $39 resort fee in addition to the room rate. It is paid directly to the resort. Ask the reservation agent whether it is charged per night or per stay so you can budget accurately.
Can I stay fewer than four nights?
The group block requires a minimum four-night stay. If you need a shorter stay, call the resort and ask about a standard reservation, but note that group pricing may not apply.
Do I have to stay at the resort to attend?
No. Everyone in the family is welcome at reunion events whether or not they stay on site. Staying at Orange Lake keeps everyone close for early breakfasts and late-night card games, but it is not required.
Where do I park and check in?
Enter from W. Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy (US-192). Check in at the main registration building, then drive to the village where your villa is located. Parking is available at each village near the villas. See the Map & Parking page for a diagram.
Which airport should I fly into?
Orlando International Airport (MCO) is the closest major airport. Rental cars and rideshare are readily available; the resort is roughly 25 miles away.
Are kids welcome? What is there for them to do?
Absolutely. The resort has pools, a lazy river, water slides, mini golf and playgrounds, and the theme parks are a short drive away. Reunion activities will include options for every age.
How do I share news about my family?
Post on the Family Board. It works like a live bulletin board: add a note or a photo and everyone sees it right away.
How do I stay in the loop?
Sign up for the newsletter on the Contact & Newsletter page. That is where reunion updates, schedule changes and reminders go out.
